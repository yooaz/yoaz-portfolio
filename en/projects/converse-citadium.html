Converse × Citadium — illustrated campaign in Paris

A multi-format visual identity bringing illustration into a Paris retail campaign.

About the project

For Converse × Citadium, YOAZ adapted a vibrant illustrated universe to a campaign with multiple placements. The visual motifs retain their impact across different shapes, formats and environments.

This selection brings together campaign imagery, poster details and design variations. It illustrates how bespoke artwork can move consistently through retail spaces and brand communication.
