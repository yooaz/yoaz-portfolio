Relic — Cipher Collection: from illustration to embroidery

An ornamental composition translated into textile, with meticulous graphic detail at the heart of the project.

About the project

The Cipher Collection for Relic carries YOAZ's visual language into embroidery. Symmetric forms, layered lines and small ornamental details are translated into a motif designed for apparel.

The images document the garment, its textures and the original visual composition. The collaboration shows how geometric and psychedelic artwork can be adapted to the constraints and possibilities of physical production.
