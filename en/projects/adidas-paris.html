Adidas Paris — illustrated apparel for the Champs-Élysées

Geometric illustration translated into apparel for a Paris-focused collaboration.

About the project

YOAZ created apparel illustration work for Adidas Paris, connected to the Champs-Élysées. The project combines an intricate composition and striking lines with a visual identity designed to work on fabric.

The images show the artwork as worn apparel and reveal details of the design. Moving artwork into physical products is a recurring part of YOAZ's practice across illustration, graphic design and textiles.
