PlayStation — God of War in the Paris Metro

A monumental illustrated world built around God of War and displayed in the Paris Metro.

About the project

Created for PlayStation, the God of War artwork expands illustration into a public environment. Layered shapes, creatures and ornamental details establish a strong visual narrative at scale.

This series brings together the Paris installation and closer views of the composition. It reflects YOAZ's approach to combining narrative imagery, geometry and intense graphic detail for widely recognised cultural worlds.
