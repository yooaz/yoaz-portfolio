YOAZ / Event design · Spatial illustration

Adobe MAX — illustration at an architectural scale

A geometric, colour-rich visual world designed to live beyond the screen, across the spaces of an international creative event.

About the project

For Adobe MAX, YOAZ created illustrations intended for large-format event environments. The visual world carries his geometric patterns, symmetry and ornamental detail into physical spaces.

The images in this selection show the work across an urban installation in New York, event screens and welcoming spaces. They document how an illustrative language can move from a digital artwork into monumental formats.
